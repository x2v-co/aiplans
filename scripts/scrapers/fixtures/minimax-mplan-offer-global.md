# M Plan monthly: 50% off your first month

> Learn about the offer period and rules.

During the M Plan launch, the first month of Go, Explore, and Build with monthly billing is 50% off. From the second month, your subscription renews automatically at the regular price.

## Offer period

From now through October 14

## Offer rules

* **Eligible plans**: This offer applies to Go, Explore, and Build with monthly billing. Annual billing is not included.
* **Offer price**: Pay the first-month offer price shown below. From the second month, your subscription renews at the regular price.

| Tier | First-month discount | Regular price |
| :- | :- | :- |
| Go | 50% off | \$22/month |
| Explore | 50% off | \$55/month |
| Build | 50% off | \$132/month |

* **New purchases**: Pay the first-month offer price of your chosen tier.
* **Upgrades**: When you upgrade to an M Plan monthly tier during the offer period, including upgrading from Token Plan or to a higher M Plan tier, you pay the first-month offer price of the new tier minus the remaining value of your current subscription.
* **Renewal**: After the first month, your subscription renews automatically at the regular price of your tier. If you do not want to renew, turn off auto-renewal before the current period ends. See [Upgrades and cancellation](/docs/m-plan/change-plan).
